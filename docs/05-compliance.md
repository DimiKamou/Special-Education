# Compliance posture (GDPR / EU AI Act / Greece)

**Own the Article 9 claim; do not dodge it.** The sentence "no diagnosis field, therefore no Art. 9 exposure" is wrong and the first competent DPO will say so in minute four: `initials · el:decode.rate=3 · encoding.spelling=3 · arr: KEDASY:oral_exam` is data from which health status is directly inferable, and inferred special-category data is Art. 9 data (Recital 35; CJEU C-184/20 *OT*). Say it first, on the first slide and in the DPA: *"This is Article 9 data. We minimise it to a controlled code vocabulary with no narrative and no clinical text; the school processes it under its own Art. 9(2)(g) basis grounded in Ν. 3699/2008 and Ν. 4823/2021, never consent; it is encrypted at rest in a separate schema on a separate role; no element of it ever enters a model payload; and it is strictly less exposing than the photocopied γνωμάτευση currently in a folder in the staffroom."* That posture is defensible, costs nothing, and pre-empts the ambush.

**v1 has no compliance relationship at all.** Software on a teacher's own device, operating on data he already lawfully holds and already writes in his markbook. No DPA, no DPIA, no sub-processor list, no parent notice — because there is no processing by a third party. That is correct sequencing, not deferral.

**The model payload has no identifier field by construction.** `PromptContext` is a frozen type with barriers, demand, blocks and params. No name, no initials, no student ref — routes are anonymous. "Accidentally include the name" is a compile error, not a code-review catch. The egress guard is defence in depth against the real leak vector, which is his own worksheet naming a student, and it **blocks and warns** rather than silently redacting.

**Outside AI Act Annex III point 3 by refusing features, not by arguing.** No evaluation of learning outcomes, no attainment prediction or criterion levelling, no placement/streaming/pathway advice, no proctoring, no screening or diagnostic inference, no emotion or engagement inference (Art. 5(1)(f), prohibited outright). Crucially: **the system never infers a barrier from student work, marks, behaviour, attendance or its own telemetry** — the barrier-write path accepts human assertions only, because inference is profiling under Art. 4(4) and Art. 6(3)'s final subparagraph makes any profiling system *always* high-risk with no derogation available. And the single cheapest insulation against a 3(b) "steering the learning process" reading: **code proposes route clusters, the teacher assigns every chip, and the record names the human who assigned.** The unit of output is the class artefact set, not the student. File the Art. 6(4) memo anyway — it costs a week and converts an argument into a record — and state the residual risk rather than asserting the conclusion.

**Art. 50 from day one, because it is cheap:** persistent non-dismissible AI indicator; XMP provenance plus a visible footer on **every** exported copy including the unmodified base, because a footer only on adapted copies is itself SEN marking.

**Three documents at school #1, not a pack:** (1) Art. 28 DPA with a sub-processor list at a stable URL, zero-retention and no-training terms; (2) a six-page **"DPIA input pack"** that explicitly says it is input to the *school's* DPIA — a pre-filled DPIA handed over is a category error, and a good DPO will refuse to sign one; (3) the Art. 6(4) classification memo. Defer the AI-literacy module, the child-readable notice, the model card and any EN 301 549 conformance statement — and note that shipping an AI-literacy module makes you a processor of *staff* data with its own lawful basis and retention schedule.

**Two things that are not optional:** `summative_external` hard refusal (two lines of code, and the thing that makes a DP coordinator trust the tool), and a `criteria_modification` path gated to coordinator level with a typed rationale and the eligibility consequence printed on the artefact — because IB *does* permit modified criteria for documented needs in defined circumstances, and a product that hard-refuses a legitimate institutional practice looks naive to the one person whose endorsement it needs.

**Zero secondary use.** No prompt tuning, no fine-tuning, no cross-school aggregates, not even anonymised — Art. 28(10) would make you a controller with no lawful basis. Telemetry is `{feature, strategy_id, accept/reject, latency}` with no codes and no pseudonym, separate table, separate retention.

**Corporate:** ΙΚΕ incorporated and professional indemnity / tech E&O cover in place **before the first signed contract**, ahead of PLD (EU) 2024/2853 transposing 9 December 2026, which classifies software and AI as products and adds rebuttable presumptions of defect and causation. A freelance natural person selling AI-mediated SEN advice into schools after that date is an unacceptable personal risk profile.
