# Hard constraints (from the seven-lens landscape pass)

Non-negotiables any design must honour or explicitly override.

- Diagnosis labels must never key any logic. A label may only adjust priors over a barrier vector; every emitted move traces to a barrier code that has at least one dated evidence entry (standardised score, work sample, teacher rating, or observation). No evidence, no move.
- Barriers and task demands share one controlled vocabulary (~24 codes, six channels). Matching is deterministic set intersection over that ontology; the LLM parses artefacts into the vocabulary and executes text transforms, but never selects strategies.
- Every transform passes a criterion x strand construct check before it may touch a summative. Construct relevance is a property of the criterion/strand, not the task: the same move can be legal for I&S Criterion D and invalidating for Language & literature Criterion D on the same piece of work. Fail closed on unclassified moves.
- MYP criteria, level descriptors and command terms are immutable. No generated output may rewrite, re-band, or reduce a descriptor, downgrade a command term in an assessed strand, or report a Year 5 summative against Year 3 criteria.
- Any volume-reduction transform must preserve strand coverage: union(strands_after) == union(strands_before). Cut only items that are redundant evidence of a strand, never the sole evidence of one. Requires per-item strand tagging on ingest.
- Every scaffold is typed at generation: atl_taught (temporary, with fade schedule and review date) or permanent_arrangement (with issuing authority and expiry) or universal_design. Untyped, un-faded scaffolds are not shippable output.
- Learner language profiles are per-language (Modern Greek / English / Ancient Greek), with separate barrier vectors. Greek demand analysis must use Greek-specific features (subordination depth, participial and nominalization density, genitive chains, register distance) — never Flesch-Kincaid or Lexile.
- Two renders from one source: student-facing output carries zero diagnostic or SEN marking; teacher-facing output carries the full rationale and evidence trail.
- Arrangements are modelled per issuing authority (KEDASY / IB / school) with independent evidence documents, scope and expiry, because a student in Greece can legitimately hold conflicting sets from both regimes.
- SEN data is GDPR Article 9 special-category. Pseudonymise at the boundary; no raw diagnostic text or gnomateusi content leaves the school tenant to a model endpoint without a DPA and field-level minimisation.
- Never implement learning styles / VAK, Irlen overlays, or dyslexia-specific fonts as evidence-backed options. Ship increased letter/word spacing and line-length control instead, and state the evidence position when a teacher asks.
- No student-identifying data may ever be included in an LLM payload. Enforce structurally (a PromptContext type with no name/DOB fields) plus a Presidio-based egress filter with Greek recognisers for ΑΜΚΑ/ΑΦΜ/Greek name stems, which blocks and warns rather than silently redacting.
- The learner profile is a controlled vocabulary (ICF-CY functional descriptors + Law 3699/2008 statutory categories + IB access-arrangement codes), never free-text diagnostic narrative. No ingest or storage of the γνωμάτευση scan in v1.
- The system must never infer or suggest a SEN category, diagnosis, or learner profile from student data. Inference = profiling under GDPR Art. 4(4), and AI Act Art. 6(3) final subparagraph makes any profiling system ALWAYS high-risk with no derogation available.
- The system must never score, grade, criterion-level, predict attainment, or recommend placement/streaming/pathway — those are Annex III points 3(a), 3(b) and 3(c) and would trigger the full Chapter III high-risk regime from 2 December 2027.
- Model inference runs in an EU region by default — AWS Bedrock eu-central-1 or Mistral EU — never api.anthropic.com directly (no EU inference_geo). The model endpoint must be a swappable adapter with region recorded in the audit log, so a CJEU ruling on the DPF (C-703/25 P, pending) is a config change and not a rewrite.
- Content classified as summative_external (IB eAssessment/DP exam, πανελλαδικές, προαγωγικές) must be refused transformation outright; the system returns only an access-arrangement checklist and the IBIS/statutory deadline references.
- Zero secondary use of school data — no prompt tuning, no fine-tuning, no cross-school aggregates, not even 'anonymised'. Art. 28(10) would make you a controller with no lawful basis.
- Every output passes a per-strategy accept/reject gate owned by a named human, with statutory or IB access arrangements requiring ΕΔΥ/SENCo-level approval; all of it recorded in a hash-chained append-only audit log carrying model, region, prompt template version and output hash.
- First run must reach a printable artifact in under 60 seconds from a cold start with zero student records — paste plus one free-text sentence about the room, no school/class/roster setup gate.
- docx round-trip is mandatory: parse OOXML on the way in (Python sidecar: python-docx + pandoc fallback) and emit a document that preserves his styles, tables, header and logo. Markdown-out is a product failure.
- Command term, MYP criterion and learning objective are invariant across all tiers; only access route varies. Enforce in the prompt AND verify mechanically (command-term list check, verbatim source-span string match, NER diff for invented facts) with the result shown as a fidelity badge.
- Fixed k=3 tiers plus an extension lane and an explicit 'individual handling' lane, with a minimum cluster size so no tier ever contains one student. Never expose ability-ranked tier names to students.
- Differentiate on a ~10-dimension access-barrier vector, never on diagnosis; the app must work fully for undiagnosed students and must never require a diagnosis to produce output.
- Every generated change must quote the span of his original it touched and name the barrier plus the named strategy; the diagnosis never appears in any printable output or in any justification text.
- Diff/suggestion-mode review is the default surface; accept-all is available but never the default action. Teacher edits and rejections must be captured as typed, inspectable, deletable rules — not opaque personalisation.
- SEN data is GDPR Art.9: EU hosting, derived barrier profile stored rather than the γνωμάτευση by default, role-gated diagnosis view, per-student delete, access audit log — designed in from schema v1, not retrofitted.
- Greek output quality is a blocking quality gate equal to correctness: register appropriate to the year group, correct MYP command-term translations, no λόγια drift. Build a Greek readability composite calibrated on the grade-labelled ΙΤΥΕ Διόφαντος textbook corpus rather than importing LIX/Dale-Chall.
- Scanned Greek PDFs and photos go to multimodal vision OCR, not Tesseract ell — diacritics and two-column textbook layouts break classical OCR.
- tenant_id on every app table plus FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY and SET LOCAL app.tenant_id in migration 0001 — never app-level tenant filtering, never retrofitted.
- All direct identifiers and diagnosis blobs live in a separate `pii` schema with its own DB role and keys; the pipeline's only view of a learner is a de-identified LearnerProfileSnapshot (barrier vector, bands, entitlements). No LLM call ever sees a name or γνωμάτευση text.
- Readability metrics, syllabification, command-term extraction, criteria/strand integrity, construct preservation and content coverage are deterministic code. An LLM may propose and rank, but may never be the gate.
- On mode='summative', only strategies with preserves_construct = true are eligible; command terms must be preserved by term identity, not synonym; overriding either requires an explicit audited teacher acknowledgement.
- Every DifferentiationPlan pins analysis_engine_version, strategy_library_version, prompt_bundle_version and model_id; plans and variants are immutable and reproducible months later.
- Content is a block-addressed ProseMirror AST with stable ULID `bid` on every node; variants are patch sets (VariantOp[]) against those bids, never opaque rewritten strings.
- LLM output is strict-schema structured span operations carrying strategy_id provenance; free-form document rewrites are rejected by the schema.
- Generated variants are re-measured by the same deterministic analyser and gated against the target band before publication; no self-reported reading level is ever stored or displayed.
- Diagnosis labels, γνωμάτευση text, and student names never reach a model prompt — only a pseudonymous, human-confirmed barrier vector does. No fine-tuning on any student-derived text, ever.
- The model returns span-anchored SpanEdit operations against a code-owned document model, never a rewritten document blob. Document reassembly happens in code.
- Strategy selection happens in code against a versioned corpus with declared contraindications and removes_construct fields. The model never chooses a strategy and is never allowed to name one — it emits artifacts, so name-dropping is structurally impossible rather than penalised.
- Command-term demand rank may never decrease on an assessed span (rank_out ≥ rank_in, same governed object), and tier-3 terminology retention must be ≥ 0.95. Both are blocking validators, not warnings; readability gates enforce a floor as well as a ceiling, because over-simplification is a failure.
- Every proposition in generated output must be entailed by the source spans, the profile record, or the strategy corpus. Fabrication rate 0 always; required-information loss 0 on summative assessment material.
- Never generate Ancient Greek (or Latin). Any Ancient Greek string in output must match an exact NFC-normalised substring of the source text or it is dropped; morphology comes from a real analyser, translation from a published aligned text, and the LLM writes only the Modern Greek scaffolding around it.
- All Greek text is NFC-normalised at the ingest boundary, and Greek accentuation is checked by a blocking validator (hunspell el_GR plus an explicit minimal-pair guard: πότε/ποτέ, πώς/πως, ή/η). Mixed normalisation is also a silent prompt-cache invalidator.
- No eval run without a persisted full trace: run_id, content_hash, profile_hash, plan, artifacts, every metric, model IDs, prompt versions, token counts, cost, latency. An eval number without a retrievable trace is a rumour.
- EU/Greece data residency, with ΚΕΔΑΣΥ γνωματεύσεις and diagnoses treated as GDPR Art. 9 special-category data about minors: pseudonymise at the boundary, never send raw diagnostic text or names to a model endpoint, ship a pre-written DPIA and a Greek-language DPA as product artefacts.
- Human-in-the-loop is mandatory and non-bypassable: no auto-applied adaptation, no automated assessment of students or teachers, no inference of new diagnostic labels — Greece's May 2026 Ministerial Decision bans profiling and fully automated assessment outright.
- Construct preservation is a hard gate, not a setting: every transformation must classify each task element as construct-relevant vs access-incidental, may mutate only the latter, and must emit a per-change rationale with a blockable criterion-impact flag (command term/strand must survive).
- Every generation writes an immutable audit record — profile version hash, content in, transformation, rationale, criterion-impact assertion, teacher approval, timestamp — exportable as IB inclusive-access evidence, Greek ΕΠΕ/ΕΔΥ record, and England Individual Support Plan.
- Standards are versioned data, never prompt text: MYP subject-group criteria and strands, Greek ΙΕΠ/ΠΣ, and England SEND Code of Practice tiers must be swappable packs so a second market is a data release, not a rewrite.
- Greek and English at full parity from v1 — UI, generated materials, exported policy documents, DPA and support — with Greek morphological/syntactic simplification treated as a distinct engine from English readability.
- Price per school, banded on active support profiles, never per seat: per-seat pricing punishes the cross-subject adoption the product depends on and drops ARPU into Teachmate's £2/teacher/month commodity zone.
- Build EU AI Act Annex III high-risk technical documentation, logging and risk management now even though obligations were deferred to 2 Dec 2027 by Reg (EU) 2026/1744 — the compliance artefacts are themselves the saleable product.
- Diagnosis labels are a write-only ingestion input: converted to barrier codes, never stored, never placed in a model prompt, never rendered in a learner-facing artefact.
- Output is a set of typed, reason-tagged edit operations over an immutable source document — never a regenerated document; untouched spans must be byte-identical (asserted in tests), which is also the only way polytonic Greek survives.
- Server-side invariants before any output renders: IB command terms preserved or strengthened, MYP criterion strands unchanged, top achievement level (7–8) still reachable, no task deleted. Fail closed.
- Every edit op carries access_delta and demand_delta; demand_delta defaults to 0 and any negative net demand change requires explicit typed teacher confirmation, logged.
- No personal data leaves the school tenancy in identifiable form: de-identified barrier vectors only, EU-region inference, zero-retention and no-training contractual terms, Art. 28 DPA, per-school key, full access audit log.
- Stay outside EU AI Act Annex III: no grading, no placement, no level assessment, no screening or diagnostic inference, and no emotion/engagement inference (Art. 5(1)(f) prohibited outright).
- Any suggestion touching summative or external assessment (IB inclusive access arrangements, προφορική εξέταση) is non-actionable, greyed, and routed to the inclusion lead for authorisation.
- Every generated artefact ships an extension dimension by default — same criterion at levels 7–8, conceptual transfer — not only an access layer; and a strengths field is mandatory on every profile.
- Teacher-facing only in v1: no student-facing surface, no automated parent-facing text, no automated statement that characterises a child.

# Key insights by lens

## pedagogy

- Construct relevance is criterion- and strand-level, not task-level. One MYP summative assessed on A and D can legitimately take a scaffold for A and must refuse the identical scaffold for D — e.g. spell-check is fine for Language & literature Criteria A/B/C and contaminates Criterion D, which explicitly assesses spelling accuracy. The core data structure is therefore a (move x subject_group x criterion x strand) legality matrix, not a strategy list.
- The legal line between scaffold and modification is form vs content: scaffolds that constrain the FORM of a response are legal; scaffolds that supply propositional content inside the assessed construct are not. A blank OPVL grid is legal for Criterion D; a pre-filled or limitation-naming one invalidates Diii. A planning template with headings scaffolds Bii; the same template with model content destroys it.
- In Greek, dyslexia inverts relative to English. Greek is shallow for reading and deep for spelling, so the profile is slow-but-accurate reading plus severe inflectional-ending spelling impairment (iotakismos: ι/η/υ/ει/οι). The Greek barrier vector must key on decode.RATE and encoding.SPELLING, never decode.accuracy — and English readability formulas are invalid for Greek demand scoring.
- In MYP, a scaffold with a fade plan IS ATL instruction; a scaffold without one is an access arrangement. Forcing every generated scaffold to declare fade_class plus either a fade schedule or an issuing authority converts undocumented differentiation into documented ATL development and a documented access record — which is exactly the evidence both IB evaluation and Greek EDY review demand and nobody currently has.
- Compute two outputs per run, not one: class-wide UDL uplift (moves that help this student and >=30% of the register — rewrite the base material) versus individual overlay (moves only this student needs). Teachers will make one better version plus two overlays; they will never make 28 versions. Getting this split wrong kills adoption in week three.
- Four or five bottlenecks — verbal WM, transcription automaticity, academic register, executive initiation/sequencing, evaluative threat — generate roughly 80% of the differentiation need across eleven distinct diagnostic labels. That redundancy is what makes a barrier-keyed engine tractable and a label-keyed engine contradictory (ADHD says vary and chunk, ASD says keep it identical; only a barrier vector can arbitrate).
- Affect barriers must not receive text transforms. Anxiety/EBSA and predictability need are answered by known assessment conditions, low-stakes retrieval, audience choice and graded exposure — not by chunking. Emitting a readability transform for an anxiety barrier is the most common category error in existing tools and should be structurally impossible in the engine.

## regulatory

- The AI Act analysis inverts the obvious intuition: this product can sit entirely OUTSIDE Annex III point 3 rather than needing the Art. 6(3) derogation — but only if it never evaluates learning outcomes and never infers a profile. The 'upload the γνωμάτευση and we'll detect the needs' feature, which sounds like the core value proposition, is precisely what would flip it into high-risk, because Art. 6(3)'s final subparagraph makes any profiling system always high-risk with no escape.
- Explicit consent is the wrong Article 9 basis in a school and understanding why changes the architecture: because consent can be withdrawn while the school's statutory duty to differentiate cannot, consent was never the real basis. Art. 9(2)(g) substantial public interest grounded in Law 3699/2008 and Law 4823/2021 is the right gateway — and Greece's Art. 9 implementation (Law 4624/2019 Art. 22) has no education clause, so the correct engineering response is to design the Art. 9 data out of the app entirely rather than to solve the legal question.
- CJEU C-413/23 P (EDPS v SRB, 4 Sept 2025) made identifiability recipient-relative, which is the legal foundation for the whole pseudonymisation strategy: a payload of {needs_codes, functional_descriptors, arrangements} is plausibly not personal data in the model vendor's hands. But the same judgment held pseudonymisation does not relieve transparency duties — so position it as radical minimisation, never as anonymisation, and still name the vendor in the parent privacy notice.
- The Digital Omnibus (Reg. (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 July 2026) deferred Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027 — but Art. 50 transparency still bites from 2 August 2026 and Art. 4 AI literacy has been binding on schools since 2 February 2025. The AI-literacy obligation the school cannot otherwise discharge is a sales wedge, not a burden.
- Greece's Law 5321/2026 makes the HDPA both the GDPR supervisory authority and the AI Act market surveillance authority for Annex III and Art. 50 — one regulator, one evidence pack. Build the DPIA, RoPA, DPA, Art. 6(4) classification memo and audit-log spec as a single artifact set that answers both regimes.
- The Product Liability Directive (EU) 2024/2853 transposes by 9 December 2026, classifies software and AI as products, and adds rebuttable presumptions of defect and causation where technical complexity burdens the claimant. A freelance natural person selling AI-mediated SEN advice into schools after that date carries unacceptable personal exposure — incorporate an ΙΚΕ and get PI cover before the first contract, not after the first pilot.
- An IB school in Greece runs two incompatible accommodation regimes simultaneously — ΚΕΔΑΣΥ statutory διευκολύνσεις and IB inclusive access arrangements (authorised via IBIS roughly six months before the MYP session). Merging them into one 'accommodations' field is the most likely way this product causes real harm: extra time given because the app suggested it, without IB authorisation, produces an invalid assessment and a maladministration finding.

## ux

- The trust-winning screen is not the rewrite, it is the analysis-and-grouping screen shown while generation streams: quoting his own longest sentence and naming his own command term proves the app read HIS document, which is the only thing that buys the next four minutes of attention.
- Diagnosis is input, barrier vector is the unit of differentiation. This single schema choice is what lets the app serve the undiagnosed majority, makes clustering meaningful, makes justifications professionally safe, and keeps the diagnosis off every printed page.
- The habit artifact is the annotated overlay plus tiered docx; the VIRAL artifact is the A6 accommodation card (spreads sideways to TAs, ΕΔΥ, substitutes); the INSTITUTIONAL artifact is the 'one lesson, four entry points' one-pager (spreads top-down to the MYP coordinator). Three different objects, three different adoption vectors, generated from the same pass.
- Tiering must hold the command term and criterion invariant and vary only the access route. An app that quietly turns 'evaluate' into 'describe' for the support tier is pedagogically harmful and will be banned by an IB coordinator the first time it is noticed — so the invariant needs mechanical verification, not just prompt wording.
- Profiles will never be maintained by hand. The only durable update mechanism is progressive profiling from the teacher's own tier overrides: five drags from Core to Guided on reading-heavy tasks is better evidence than a three-year-old γνωμάτευση, and it is a real moat because no incumbent captures it.
- The 'no document, just a topic' input path has zero friction and accounts for 20-30% of real use, yet it is missing from every input-path list. Ship it in v1 alongside docx.
- Learned preferences must be exposed as plain-language, deletable rules. A φιλόλογος who must professionally own the output will accept a system whose memory he can read and edit, and will abandon one that silently changes its behaviour.

## architecture

- The moat is not diagnosis→strategy — it is a DemandChannel vocabulary shared by both the learner model and the content analyser, so a strategy fires only where the learner's barrier collides with a demand the analyser actually measured in THIS text. Freeze that 17-value enum first; everything (retrieval, prompts, validation, provenance UI, effectiveness analytics) hangs off it.
- Roughly 40% of a good strategy library is render_directive or deterministic_transform — 14pt, 1.5 leading, ≤65-char measure, one question per page, numbered steps, bolded command term, sentence-per-line. Zero LLM tokens, zero hallucination surface, and these are the changes teachers notice first. Model execution_kind explicitly or you will pay Opus rates to change a font size.
- IB fidelity reduces to one hard database-enforced rule: on summative content only strategies with preserves_construct = true may apply, and command terms must be preserved by identity not synonym (analyse→describe is a criterion-level change, not a simplification). Access arrangements are entitlements applied unconditionally; strategies are choices. Two different entities.
- Flesch-Kincaid does not port to Greek, but Greek syllabification does — it is rule-deterministic (~99% with 120 lines), so polysyllable/SMOG-style indices transfer while character-based ones do not. The real Greek difficulty signal is the triad: genitive-chain depth (γενική συσσώρευση), nominalisation density (-ση/-μός/-τητα), and learned participles (-θείς, -ων/-ούσα). Calibrate an ordinal model against ebooks.edu.gr's grade-labelled textbook corpus rather than importing any formula.
- The LLM must emit span-anchored VariantOps against stable block ULIDs under a strict schema — never a rewritten document. That single choice buys provenance, diffing, partial acceptance, per-block regeneration, cheap tokens, and the hover-to-explain UI that converts a SENCO from sceptic to advocate.
- Nobody builds the answer-leak check, and LLM scaffolds give the answer away constantly. Extract a claim list / mark-scheme entailment check and make it a hard gate. Same category: never trust a model's claim that it simplified text — re-run the same deterministic analyser on the output and gate on the measurement.
- Per-student differentiation dies on contact with 5 classes × 25 students. The daily-use unit is cohort mode: cluster the class on barrier vectors into 3 tiers and explicitly name the coverage gap ("these 2 students still need an individual version"). Ship it by month 3 or the tool stays a demo.

## ai

- The label is the worst possible conditioning variable and must be removed from the prompt entirely. Parse the diagnosis once, under human confirmation, into a ~30-dimension ordinal barrier vector; that vector is the only thing that conditions generation. This simultaneously fixes the 'same five bullets per label' failure, covers undiagnosed and EAL students, and solves GDPR Article 9 exposure at the architecture level rather than the policy level.
- Ship profile-sensitivity and content-sensitivity as blocking regression metrics. Same content × 5 archetype profiles: median pairwise strategy-set Jaccard must be < 0.45. Same profile × 5 contents: Jaccard < 0.5. Nobody ships these, and they are the only metrics that directly measure the actual disease (generic sludge) rather than a proxy for it.
- Access vs. modification — Messick's construct-irrelevant vs construct-relevant variance — is the invariant that makes the product defensible to an IB coordinator, and it must be encoded as a constraint solver over per-criterion protected constructs, not as a prompt instruction. Every strategy card declares removes_construct[]; selection is refused in code when that intersects the task's assessed_constructs. The 'refused, and here is why' list is a headline product feature, not debug output.
- Span-anchored SpanEdit output (the model returns typed edit operations against a code-owned document model, never a rewritten document) is the single highest-leverage architectural decision. Validation, diffing, artifact caching, teacher-edit capture, and the entire flywheel all fall out of that one representation for free.
- Cost per additional profile is dominated not by prompt caching but by two structural moves: splitting the demand analysis into a profile-independent pass cached by content hash, and content-keyed artifact reuse whose key excludes the profile and includes only quantised strategy parameters. SEN profiles in one class cluster hard — expect 30–45% artifact reuse on profiles 2–4.
- Judge the judge before trusting it: seed 10 known-bad artifacts per eval run (demand downgrade, fabricated fact, stripped tier-3 term, accent error, construct-removing scaffold) and only trust a rubric dimension at seeded-defect recall > 0.9 and Cohen's κ ≥ 0.6 against the builder's own ratings. Everything else stays human-only and you say so out loud.
- Greek's ~2.5× token penalty lands almost entirely on the input side, which caches at 0.1× — so it strengthens rather than weakens the case for a frontier model on Greek generation. The real Greek risks are accentuation errors on minimal pairs and confident Ancient Greek fabrication, both of which are deterministic post-checks, not prompting problems.

## market

- The two adjacent incumbents each own exactly half the problem and neither is crossing: Edukey Provision Map (from £795/yr) explicitly does NOT generate strategies or adapt content, while MagicSchool/Diffit/Brisk adapt content but hold no durable student model. The unoccupied seam between the SEN record and the instructional artefact is the entire product.
- Willingness to pay tracks legal risk, not time saved. SENCO AI charges £500/month (£6,000/yr) for consultation letters and EHCNA drafts; Provision Map £795/yr for a database; time-saving teacher tools sit at €6/month. Sell the evidence record as risk reduction and you price 10-20x above the productivity category.
- Four independent compliance forcing functions landed simultaneously in 2026: Ofsted's 7 Sept 2026 toolkit makes inclusion a standalone graded evaluation area on a public five-point report card; the Schools White Paper creates statutory Individual Support Plans for the 1.32M pupils at SEN Support with no EHCP; IB requires IBIS access-arrangement submissions 6 months ahead; Greece's May 2026 Ministerial Decision mandates supervision and bans profiling. All four demand documented adaptation and all four leave the documenting manual.
- Greece is putting the IB Diploma into ~15 public Lykeia from September 2026, state-funded, with 500+ already-trained teachers — a once-a-decade market-creation event for a Greek-speaking, EU-hosted, IB-fluent inclusion tool, and the builder is an MYP teacher, Greek philologist and engineer simultaneously.
- MagicSchool ($65.3M raised, 13,000+ schools) hosts in U.S. data centres with FERPA/COPPA framing. For EU minors' special-category health data that is a structural, expensive-to-reverse disqualifier — the cleanest wedge a solo builder will ever get against a well-funded incumbent.
- Curipod's 2026 pivot away from generic AI lesson generation toward writing practice is the market telling you that 'generate materials' is not a durable business. The generation is the bait; the persistent profile and the evidence ledger are the asset.

## risk

- The killing metric is verification cost, not editing cost. A regenerated document forces O(document) re-reading every time; an accept/reject diff of reason-tagged edit ops makes it O(changes). This single architectural choice decides adoption, protects Greek polytonic and .docx fidelity, makes anti-dumbing-down invariants checkable, and produces the only good preference signal you will get.
- Conditioning generation on a diagnosis label is what builds the deficit machine — the model's label→template prior overwhelms any system prompt, and within-label heterogeneity exceeds between-label variance, so output is simultaneously generic and wrong while feeling personalised. Barrier codes as the profile primitive defuse the pedagogical, DPO, parent, missing-data and undiagnosed-majority failures at once.
- The no-diagnosis version is the better product, not the degraded fallback: it is evidence-aligned (EEF five-a-day, UDL 3.0, IB's four inclusion principles are all label-independent), keeps you out of Art. 9 special-category data, covers the undiagnosed majority that ΚΕΔΑΣΥ waiting lists leave unserved, and has no SENCo, DPO or parent gate — so it can ship to any teacher tomorrow, with the student-linked version as the institutional upsell.
- The SENCo is the single most powerful veto in the building and the single best sponsor. Make her the sole author of student-linked profiles and constrain student-linked generation to retrieval over a school-approved strategy bank — the app becomes her distribution channel for ILPs nobody currently reads, which is a capability she has never had.
- Simplification collapses reading demand and conceptual demand together unless you split them into two typed axes. 'Evaluate the extent to which…' silently becoming 'List three…' is undetectable by inspection and is the actual stigma-and-expectations failure; the fix is an enforced demand ledger plus command-term and criterion-strand invariance, not a better prompt.
- Screening is the most tempting and most indefensible feature in the roadmap. 'These barriers suggest possible dyslexia' is a ΚΕΔΑΣΥ competence in Greece, an Annex III/Art. 22 exposure in the EU, and terminal the first time it is wrong about a child. Write the prohibition into the prompt, an output filter, and the roadmap.
